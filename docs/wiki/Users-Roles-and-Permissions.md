# Users, Roles and Permissions

## Permission catalog

Permissions are `module.action` keys (`src/server/rbac.ts`):

| Module | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| `vendors` | Vendor records and ledgers |
| `customers` | Customer records and ledgers |
| `products` | Products, categories, brands, stock page, adjustments, stock counts and transfers (`products.edit`) |
| `purchases` | Purchase invoices, CSV import, vendor payments and purchase returns |
| `sales` | POS, invoices, customer payments, due collection, customer returns |
| `serials` | Serial lookup, labels, service tickets |
| `accounts` | Accounts, payments, income and expense, transfers, dues |
| `reports` | Reports hub, CSV exports, cost/profit visibility |
| `users` | Users and roles |
| `settings` | Company settings and branches |

Each module has the actions `view`, `create`, `edit` and `delete`. Two more keys:

- `activity.view`: the activity log.
- `branches.all`: see every branch and switch the working branch (see [Multi-Branch](Multi-Branch)).

That is 42 keys in total. Every server action and data route checks the signed-in user's permission **before** doing
anything; hiding a button is never the only control.

## Default roles

| Role | Permissions | Editable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything (system administrator flag) | No. At least one active admin must always remain. |
| **Manager** | Every key except `users.delete` and `settings.delete`; includes `branches.all` | Yes |
| **Salesman** | `sales.view`, `sales.create`, `customers.view`, `customers.create`, `serials.view`, `serials.create` | Yes |
| Custom | Built on the **Roles** page, or cloned from an existing role | Yes |

A role cloned from Manager does **not** automatically get `branches.all` in companies upgraded from before branches
existed; grant it on the Roles page if those users should switch branches.

## Who may manage whom (no escalation)

The administrator can do everything. Anyone else with `users.*` permissions is limited by the **strict-subset rule**:

- They can create, edit, clone or assign only a role whose permissions are **strictly fewer** than their own: a subset
  of what they hold, missing at least one key.
- They can manage only users whose permissions are a strict subset of their own. This blocks a peer takeover: two
  users with the same permissions cannot reset each other's passwords.
- They cannot edit their own role, and cannot touch an admin account.
- The role editor warns when a selection equals your own permissions, and **Clone** appears only for roles whose copy
  you could create.
- Only keys in the current catalog count; a retired key stored on an old role never counts as an extra permission.

## Users

**Administration > Users & Roles** (`users.view`):

- **Create** (`users.create`): name, email, role, branch and an initial password (typed, at least 10 characters, or
  generated by the server).
- **Edit** (`users.edit`): role, branch, details; **reset password**.
- **Deactivate / reactivate** (`users.delete`): users are never deleted, only deactivated, so the history keeps who
  did what. A user cannot be reactivated into an inactive branch.
- **Your own account** (`/account`) shows your details; you can change your own password at `/account/password`.
  Name, email and role are changed by someone with `users.edit` on the Users page. Changing **your own** sign-in email
  there asks for your current password.

## When users are signed out

Each user has a session version. These changes bump it, and every existing session of that user stops working on the
next request:

- changing the user's role, or the permissions of their role,
- changing the user's branch,
- resetting or changing the password,
- deactivating the account.

Sessions otherwise last **7 days**. Changing `AUTH_SECRET` signs everyone out.

## Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost 12); older cost-10 hashes are upgraded on the next successful sign-in.
- Minimum 10 characters; at most 72 bytes (a bcrypt limit).
- Sign-in is throttled: 5 failures per 15 minutes per email and IP, and 20 per 15 minutes per email from any IP. See
  [Security Model](Security-Model).
- Forgotten admin password: `npm run admin:reset -- --email you@example.com` (see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting)).

## Who sees costs

- **Profit visibility** (`reports.view` or `sales.edit`) is needed to see stock value and net costs on `/stock` and
  product pages, the purchase price on `/products`, cost and profit columns in reports and CSVs, the dashboard stock
  value, and unit cost and profit on serial pages. The fields are left out on the server, not just hidden.
- `purchases.view` shows purchase costs where they are the document itself: purchase invoices, purchase returns, the
  purchase import, and a vendor's supplied units.
- Reports that read the accounts (profit and loss, cash flow) also need `accounts.view`.

## Activity log

**Administration > Activity Log** (`activity.view`) lists who changed what and when, with before/after values.
Fields whose names look like secrets are dropped before logging (see [Security Model](Security-Model)). In a company
with more than one branch, the activity log and the change history of shared records also need `branches.all`.
